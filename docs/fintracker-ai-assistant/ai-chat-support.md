# FinTracker Analytics - User AI Assistant

## 1: User AI Assistant

### Problem
Many users already run their daily life through a general AI assistant, alongside their calendar, email and documents. Their finances are locked inside FinTracker, so they copy numbers or paste screenshots and statements into the assistant. That is error-prone, and it's insecure because pasted statements expose account numbers and personal details.

For users, answers built on manual copying are wrong or out of date, and sensitive data gets shared carelessly or it's becoming an app people open rarely when more people prefer using AI Assistant for their daily tasks. 


### Why the user's own assistant: 
It meets users where they already are and combines their finances with their other tools, such as planning a trip budget against their calendar. FinTracker pays no AI model cost, because the user's subscription covers it.

Limits:

Only data the user explicitly allows is shared, read-only by default.
Users can disconnect at any time and see every request their assistant made.
Only summarized facts are shared, never account numbers or identity details.
FinTracker is responsible for the accuracy of the data it provides, not for how the assistant interprets it. This must be stated clearly to users.
Success measures: the share of users who connect, weekly active connected users, retention compared with users who haven't connected, paid conversion, and zero security incidents.
