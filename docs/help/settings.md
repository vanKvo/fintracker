## What you can change

Settings is where you manage your timezone and your custom categories. Other items in Settings may appear read-only. If a field is read-only, it cannot be changed in the app yet.

## Change your timezone

Your timezone decides what "today" and "this month" mean, and when bills are due. If you travel or move, change it here.

1. Open Settings.
2. Choose your timezone.
3. Save.

If saving fails you see "Could not save. Your previous setting is unchanged."

## Currency and language

Currency is USD and cannot be changed. English is the only language. Transactions in another currency are not counted in totals.

## Custom categories

FinTracker comes with system categories such as Groceries and Dining. You can add your own, for example "Auto Property Tax". Your custom categories are visible only to you.

### Rules for category names

A name can use letters, numbers and spaces only. Spaces and capital letters are tidied automatically. A name cannot match an existing system category or one of your own. Names have a maximum length, and you can have up to 50 custom categories.

If a name is rejected, FinTracker tells you why: not allowed characters, a name that already exists, or the limit reached.

### Rename or remove a category

Renaming a category updates every transaction and budget that uses it. Removing a custom category does not delete it from your history. It is deactivated, so past transactions keep it, but you cannot choose it for new transactions or budgets.

## Profile

Your name and email are shown for reference. If they cannot be edited, they are read-only.

## Close your account

You can close your account. Sign-in is disabled right away, and your data is kept for a 30-day grace period during which support can reactivate it. After that, your transactions, statements and settings are permanently deleted.
