# FinTracker Overview

FinTracker is a personal finance app. You upload bank statements, review the imported transactions, set monthly budgets, and see where your money goes. This page is a map of the app: what each tab is for and which tab to open for a given task.

## The seven tabs

FinTracker has seven tabs: Dashboard, Transactions, Statements, Budgets, Reports, Accounts and Settings.

- **Dashboard** shows this month only: a snapshot of where you stand right now.
- **Transactions** lists every transaction. You review, approve, categorize, split and exclude them here.
- **Statements** is where you upload bank statements and manage the ones you have already uploaded.
- **Budgets** is where you set monthly spending limits per category and track progress.
- **Reports** shows past periods: income vs expenses, spending by category, top merchants and an AI-written Financial Summary.
- **Accounts** lists the bank accounts that your statements and transactions belong to.
- **Settings** holds your timezone and your custom categories.

## Where do I find...?

Use this list to go straight to the right tab.

- **Upload a bank statement:** Statements tab. See "Upload a statement".
- **Approve or categorize imported transactions:** Transactions tab. Imported rows start as pending until you approve them.
- **Add a cash purchase by hand:** Transactions tab, using the Add Transaction button.
- **See how much I have spent this month:** Dashboard tab.
- **Compare this month's spending with last month at the same point:** Dashboard tab, spending pace card.
- **Set or change a monthly budget:** Budgets tab.
- **See last month, the last 3 months, year to date or the last 12 months:** Reports tab.
- **Read the AI Financial Summary:** Reports tab.
- **See my top merchants or spending by category:** Reports tab.
- **Delete a statement:** Statements tab. Its transactions are deleted with it.
- **Add or remove a bank account:** Accounts tab.
- **Create a custom category:** Settings tab.
- **Change my timezone:** Settings tab.

## Why some numbers differ between tabs

Dashboard covers the current month. Reports covers complete past periods and has no "This month" option. Pending transactions are not counted anywhere until you approve them. See "Key concepts" for the full explanation.

## Signing in and out
