# Sign In and Your Session

## Sign in

Open FinTracker and choose the sign-in button on the login page. You are taken to a secure sign-in page, and FinTracker never sees your password. After you sign in you land on the Dashboard.

If you do not have an account yet, use the register button on the same page. Registration also happens on the secure sign-in page.

## Choose your timezone on first sign-in

FinTracker needs a timezone so that "this month", "today" and bill due dates match your calendar. The first time you sign in, FinTracker saves your browser's timezone automatically.

If your browser does not report a timezone, a picker opens and you must choose one before you can continue. It cannot be closed without a choice. You can change your timezone later in Settings.

## Idle sign-out

For your security, FinTracker signs you out after a period of inactivity. After 13 minutes with no activity, a dialog warns you and starts a 2-minute countdown.

- Choose **Stay signed in** to keep working. The timer resets.
- If the countdown reaches zero, or you choose **Sign out**, you are signed out.

## Sign out

Choose Sign out from the app. Signing out ends your session on every device where you are signed in, not only the one you are using. You can sign back in at any time.

## If you cannot load a page

If a page is slow or shows an error, use the Retry button on the part that failed. Other parts of the page keep working. If the first load after a long quiet period is slow, wait a moment and retry once.

## Closing your account

You can ask to close your account. Closing disables sign-in right away and starts a 30-day grace period. During those 30 days, contact support to reactivate it and restore all your data. After the grace period your data is permanently deleted.
