# Reports

The Reports tab shows complete past periods. For the current month, use the Dashboard.

## Choose a period

One selector at the top controls the period. The options are Last month, Last 3 months, Year to date and Last 12 months. The default is Last month.

There is no "This month" option because the Dashboard covers the current month. A period with no counted transactions is disabled and shows "No data for this period".

The selector changes the Financial Summary, spending by category and top merchants. The two trend charts always show the last 12 complete months, whatever you select.

The page labels the comparison, for example "Jul–Sep 2026 vs. Apr–Jun 2026".

## Financial Summary

The Financial Summary is a short written review of your selected period, compared with the period before it. It covers income, expenses, net cash flow, your top categories and notable changes. The numbers come from your approved transactions, and an AI writes the explanation around them.

The summary always ends with "Informational only. Not financial advice." It gives budgeting observations only, not investment, tax or credit advice.

### Refresh the summary

You can refresh the summary a limited number of times each day. The page shows how many refreshes remain. At zero the button is disabled and reads "No refreshes left today. Try again tomorrow."

### If the AI summary is unavailable

If the AI cannot produce a summary, you see a facts-only version with your key numbers and a short status for each. Try again later for the written version.

## Income vs expenses by month

A chart of income, expenses and net for each of the last 12 complete months. Expenses are spending after refunds. A month with no data shows as a gap, not as zero spending.

## Spending by category

Lists each category with its spending in the selected period, the previous amount, and the change in amount and percent. Categories are sorted from highest spending. Uncategorized spending appears as its own row.

## Category trends

Shows how each category changed month by month over the last 12 complete months. Switch categories in the chart without reloading.

## Top merchants

Lists the 10 merchants where you spent the most in the selected period, with the number of transactions and each merchant's share of total spending.

## What is counted in reports

Reports include approved transactions only. Pending transactions, excluded transactions, transfers and adjustments are not counted. Refunds reduce spending in their category. Transactions in a currency other than USD are left out, and the page notes it.

## Export

Exporting reports to CSV or PDF is not available yet.

## If a section fails to load

If one section fails, it shows an error and the rest of the page stays. If the whole page fails, the previous charts stay with a Retry button.
