# Accounts

The Accounts tab lists the bank accounts that your statements and transactions belong to, such as a checking account or a credit card.

## Why accounts matter

Every statement and every manual transaction belongs to one account. When you upload a statement you choose its account, and an account can have only one statement per calendar month.

## Add an account

Choose the option to add or link a new account, give it a name, and choose its type. You need an account before you can upload a statement for it.

## Account numbers

FinTracker keeps only the last 4 digits of an account number, and shows them as `•••• 1234`. The "Last 4 digits" field is optional. If you paste a longer number, only its last 4 digits are used, and the rest is never sent or stored. FinTracker never needs your full account or card number.

## Edit an account

Open an account to change its name or details. Changing an account's name updates it everywhere it is shown.

## Accounts and your data

Transfers between two of your own accounts are not counted as spending or income. FinTracker treats them as money moving, not money earned or spent.

## Remove an account

Removing an account also removes the transactions that belong to it. Review the account's statements before you remove it.
