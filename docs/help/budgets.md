A budget is a monthly spending limit for each category, such as $500 for Groceries. FinTracker shows how much you have spent against each limit.

## Create a budget

Open the Budgets tab and choose a month. The current month is shown by default. If the month has no budget, you choose how to start:

- **From a template:** pick a ready-made template such as Basic Living or Aggressive Savings, or one of your own.
- **From last month:** copy the categories and limits from your most recent budget.
- **From scratch:** start empty and add categories yourself.

Each month has one budget.

## Add, change or remove a category

On an open budget you can add a category with a limit, change a limit, or remove a category. A budget can have up to 50 categories, and a category can appear only once. Limits can have up to two decimal places.

Removing a category from a budget removes the limit only. Your transactions are not touched.

## How spending is counted

Spent amounts include approved transactions only. Pending transactions are not counted until you approve them. Refunds reduce the spending in their category, and transfers are not counted. Excluded transactions are not counted.

For a future month, spent is zero because nothing has happened yet.

## Past months are read-only

A budget closes automatically at the start of the next month, and a closed budget cannot be edited or deleted. This keeps your history accurate. You can still read it. The current month and future months can be edited.

If you need to change a closed month, it must be reopened first.

## Move between months

Use the month selector to move between months. Opening a month that has no budget never creates one by itself. You choose how to start it.

## Save a budget as a template

You can save a budget's categories and limits as your own template, with a name that is unique among your templates. Using a template copies its limits into the new budget, so later changes to the budget do not change the template.

## Delete a budget

You can delete the budget of an open month. This removes the limits only. Your transactions stay.

## Budget alerts

When a category is near or over its limit, it appears in the Budget alerts section of the Dashboard.
