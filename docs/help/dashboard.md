The Dashboard is a snapshot of the current month. It has no date selector and no charts. For past months and charts, use the Reports tab.

## What the Dashboard shows

The Dashboard has these sections, and each loads on its own:

- **Data through date:** the date of the latest transaction counted in the totals.
- **Summary cards:** this month's totals.
- **Spending pace:** how much you have spent so far this month compared with the same days last month.
- **Budget alerts:** categories that are near or over their monthly limit.
- **Upcoming bills:** bills due this month.
- **Recent transactions:** your latest transactions.

## Data through date and pending transactions

The "data through" date tells you how current the totals are. If you have pending transactions, the Dashboard shows a note such as "12 awaiting approval aren't in these totals".

Pending transactions are imported rows you have not approved yet. They are not counted until you approve them in the Transactions tab. If your Dashboard total looks too low after an upload, approve the pending rows.

## Spending pace

The spending pace card compares your spending from the 1st of this month through today with the same days of last month. It shows the amount for each, the difference and the percent change.

If you had no spending in the same days last month, the card shows no percent change because there is nothing to compare against.

## Budget alerts

Budget alerts list the categories where your spending is close to or over the monthly limit you set in Budgets. If you have no budget for the month, or nothing is near a limit, this section is hidden.

## Upcoming bills

This section lists bills due this month. If none are due, it shows "No bills due this month". You can mark a bill as paid from here.

## Recent transactions

Shows your most recent transactions for the month. To see everything, filter, or take action on a row, open the Transactions tab.

## If a section fails to load

One failing section never blanks the page. A section that cannot load shows an error and a Retry button. Retry reloads only that section.

## Empty Dashboard

If you have not uploaded any statements yet, the Dashboard shows empty states, not sample data. Upload a statement in the Statements tab to get started.
