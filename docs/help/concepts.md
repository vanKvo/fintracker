# Key Concepts

<!-- verify: wording for "excluded" and "approved" matches the UI labels (Pending / Approved / Posted). -->

This page explains the ideas behind FinTracker's numbers. Most "why doesn't this match?" questions are answered here.

## Pending, approved and counted

An imported transaction starts as **pending**. After you approve it, it is **approved**. Only approved transactions are counted in the Dashboard totals, budgets and reports. Manual transactions you add are counted right away.

If your totals look too low, check for pending transactions in the Transactions tab and approve them.

## Excluded transactions

Excluding a transaction keeps it in your list but removes it from budgets and reports. Use it for rows you do not want counted. The record is not deleted, and turning Exclude off brings it back.

## Transaction types and how they are counted

Every transaction has one of five types:

- **Expense:** money out. Counts as spending.
- **Income:** money in, such as salary. Counts as income.
- **Refund:** money back from a purchase. It reduces spending in its category and does not count as income.
- **Transfer:** money moved between your own accounts. It is not counted as spending or income.
- **Adjustment:** a correction. It is not counted in spending totals.

A refund larger than the spending in its category shows as negative spending for that category.

## Why Dashboard and Reports can differ

The Dashboard covers the current month. Reports covers complete past periods and has no "This month". If you compare them for different periods, the numbers will differ. For the same period, both count approved transactions the same way, so a difference usually means pending or excluded rows.

## Your timezone defines your month

"This month", "today" and bill due dates use the timezone saved in Settings, not your device's clock. If a transaction near midnight on the last day of a month appears in the wrong month, check your timezone.

## Uncategorized transactions

If FinTracker cannot place a transaction in a category, it is saved as Uncategorized. Uncategorized spending is still counted in totals. Change its category in the Transactions tab and it moves to that category in budgets and reports.

## One statement per account per month

An account can have only one statement per calendar month, taken from the statement's end date. Uploading another for the same account and month asks whether to overwrite.

## Data through date

The "data through" date on the Dashboard is the latest transaction date counted in your totals. If it is behind today, upload your latest statement.

## Your data is private

You see only your own transactions, statements, budgets and custom categories. Other users cannot see them.
