## Manage your statements

The Statements tab lists every statement you have uploaded. Each row shows the date range, the account, the number of transactions, how many are pending and approved, and the status.

### View a statement's transactions

Choose View Transactions on a row to open the Transactions tab already filtered to that statement's account and date range. Use this to check that the imported rows match your bank statement.

### Approve all pending transactions

Choose Approve all pending on a row to approve every pending transaction from that statement in one step. Approved rows are then counted in your budgets and reports. If some rows cannot be approved, for example because a category is no longer available, FinTracker tells you how many were approved and how many were not, and the others stay pending.

### Download a statement

Choose Download to get the original file you uploaded. If the download link has expired, choose Download again to get a new one.

### Delete a statement

Choose Delete on a row to remove a statement. Deleting a statement also deletes all the transactions that came from it. This cannot be undone, and the transactions disappear from your budgets and reports.

Use Delete when you uploaded the wrong file or want to upload a corrected version. To remove only one transaction, use Exclude in the Transactions tab instead.

### Replace a statement with a corrected file

You cannot keep two statements for the same account and month. Upload the new file and choose Overwrite in the duplicate dialog. The old statement and its transactions are deleted and the new file is imported.

### Check which months are missing

Look through the statement list by month for gaps. If a month is missing for an account, its transactions are not in FinTracker and your totals for that month will be too low. Upload that month's statement to fill the gap.
